Yingqian Cui's Homepage

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Michigan State University. I am co-advised by Prof. Jiliang Tang and Prof. Yue Xing. I received my B.Sc. (Honors) in Mathematics and Statistics from Hong Kong Baptist University.

My research focuses on the reasoning capabilities of large language models, with interests spanning efficient and effective reasoning, theoretical understanding of in-context learning and chain-of-thought, and trustworthy AI.
